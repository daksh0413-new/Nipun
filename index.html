Rooted in Heritage, Driven by Purpose

Inspired by pioneering welfare trusts like the Sir Dorabji Tata Trust (sdtt.in) and community foundations, KWS focuses on sustainable rural livelihood models that allow traditional artisans to retain ownership of their heritage while reaching modern domestic and global export markets.

Self Help Group Mobilization

Forming decentralized village-level micro-enterprises where women produce sacred havan samagri, dhoop batti, temple flower incense, and textile arts.

GI Tag & ODOP Preservation

Protecting geographical identity credentials for Mathura metalcraft, Sanjhi paper stencils, and regional indigenous incense formulations.

UPSDM Certified Training

Formalizing artisan skills via certified curriculum under the Uttar Pradesh Skill Development Mission to guarantee fair market wages and dignified employment.

Sacred Fragrances & Spiritual Ecology

Braj Surbhi: Pure Aromas from the Divine Groves of Vrindavan

Braj Surbhi is the signature socio-spiritual initiative created by Khajani Welfare Society. By collecting sacred temple flower offerings from historic pilgrimage shrines across Mathura, Vrindavan, and Govardhan, our rural women artisans transform floral botanicals into chemical-free, charcoal-free dhoop, havan samagri, and essential incense.

Charcoal-Free Dhoop

Zero black carbon smoke. Gentle on lungs and ideal for daily home pooja.

Ready Havan Cups

Pure cow dung charcoal cups infused with loban, camphor, and cow ghee.

Natural Itra & Attars

Hydro-distilled pure rose, sandalwood, and khus essences from regional stills.

Direct Artisan Income

100% of proceeds directly fund our women artisan cooperatives.
